**Thesis submission:** May–June 2026 · **Defence:** September–October 2026 (can be **remote**) · **Availability for postdoc:** from **July 2026** onward (after thesis submission) · [Profile](https://www.cgs.iitk.ac.in/user/tapasr/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/tapasiitk)

I am a **cognitive scientist** trained in **behavioural experimentation, computational modelling, and statistical inference**. My Ph.D. examines **context effects in human decision-making**—how decoys and attribute structure bias choice—and how to **stress-test measurement tools** (including via simulation and agent-based models) so claims stay robust under heterogeneous preferences.

I am extending this programme into **human–AI interaction**, with a focus on **sense of agency (SoA)** and **ownership of action** when people use algorithmic and **agentic** systems. My longer-term framework, **Proxy Agency**, asks what happens when assistance is fluent enough that AI-mediated actions feel continuous with one’s own will—and how that interacts with **ethical responsibility** and **moral shielding** (implicit endorsement of harmful or aggressive outcomes mediated by the system). My core strength is **designing and running human-in-the-loop studies** with tight stimulus control and appropriate hierarchical modelling—not training frontier RL systems.

My background is **B.Tech. in Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering**, followed by industry experience in **large-scale software QA** (Accenture / British Telecom). That training supports **instrument-grade experiments** (timing, implementation, reproducibility) and **building custom paradigms** (e.g. granular control via JavaScript; PsychoPy; **eye tracking with Eyelink 1000 Plus**). For analysis I rely on **R** (e.g. `lme4`, `brms`) and **Python** (Pandas, SciPy). I have **self-taught foundations of reinforcement learning** and use **Python/PyTorch** to build **computational benchmarks** for human adaptability (e.g. DRQN-style agents in POMDP-style tasks). I want to **deepen ML and data-structures literacy** where it improves human-centred inference—not to rebrand as a MARL engineer.

## Ph.D. contributions (behavioural + modelling core)

- **Empirical:** Perceptual and multi-attribute decision-making; **attraction / decoy** effects; rigorous treatment of asymmetries and paradigms (e.g. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 2025; *CogSci* 2025; *PsyArXiv* preprint on triplet–triplet metrics; manuscripts submitted / in prep. — see profile for full list).  
- **Methods:** Custom online and lab-ready tasks; emphasis on **statistical robustness** and **simulation** to evaluate whether standard indices behave sensibly under realistic heterogeneity.  
- **Modelling thread:** Bounded rationality and **strategy selection** under resource constraints; linking human choice patterns to formal frameworks where appropriate.

This work is the base I use to approach HAI: the same care for **task design**, **confounds**, and **measurement validity** transfers directly to studies of automation, recommendation, and delegation.

## Human–AI direction (near-term agenda)

- **Sense of agency in recommender-style settings:** how algorithmic ranking affects the **intention–action link** and perceived control.  
- **Agentic AI and delegation:** how **level of automation**, **fluency**, and **framing** shift attributions of control and responsibility—setting up behavioural tests of **Proxy Agency** and related moral-psychology predictions.  
- **Optional bridges:** multiplayer / interactive digital environments as ecologically richer contexts for agency and accountability (aligned with interests listed on my profile).

Experiments—not simulator scale—is the rate-limiting step I intend to own in a postdoc.
